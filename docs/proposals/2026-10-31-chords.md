# Proposal: chord steps (e.g. LP+MP)

## Context
SPEC §9 lists simultaneous multi-button presses as out of scope, and the Backlog ("Advanced combo-building mode → Grouped inputs") asks for them. Chords change the engine's `Step` type and grading, so CLAUDE.md requires a written proposal and spec update before any code. This document is that proposal. **Scope: button chords only.** No code is written until you answer the DECISION items.

---

## The worked example (used in every option below)

- 60 fps, relative mode. The previous step landed on some grid frame; call that **frame 0** for this step.
- Chord step: **LP+MP**, `targetFrame` 10, `tolEarly` 1, `tolLate` 1 → on-time frames **9, 10, 11**.
- "W" = chord window (how many frames apart the two presses may be).

| Case | Presses (frames after reference) | Apart | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | LP 10, MP 10 | 0 | Q1, Q2 |
| S2 | LP 10, MP 11 | 1 | Q1, Q2 |
| S3 | LP 10, MP 13 | 3 | Q1, Q2 |
| S4 | LP 7, MP 10 (LP 3f early, MP on target) | 3 | Q3 |
| S5 | LP 8, MP 13 (LP 1f past early edge → −2; MP +3) | 5 | Q4 |
| S6 | LP 10, nothing else | — | Q5 |
| S7 | LP 10, HP 10 | 0 | Q5 |
| S8 | LP 10, MP 10, HP 10 | 0 | Q5 |

A single-button step behaves exactly as today in every option (a chord of one has no window and nothing to combine). All existing grading tests stay valid as the regression guard.

## DECISION 1 — Chord window

"Frames apart" = difference between the two presses' **grid frames** (the same fixed grid grading uses).

| Option | What it is | S1 | S2 | S3 | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1A. Same frame only (W = 0)** | Both presses must land on the same grid frame | chord | not a chord → Q5 | not a chord → Q5 | Simplest, strictest. Grid-phase sensitive: two presses 2 ms apart that straddle a frame edge count as 1 frame apart and fail. On keyboard, two hand-pressed keys often land more than a frame apart. On a pad, buttons seen in the same poll share one timestamp, so they're always the same frame. |
| **1B. One fixed window for the whole app** (e.g. W = 3, a constant or a `Settings` field like strict mode) | Second press within W frames of the first | chord | chord | chord (3 apart) | One number to learn, no editor field. Can't match a specific game's leniency per combo. If it's a Settings field, it's saved per browser, so a shared combo can grade differently on another machine. |
| **1C. Adjustable per step** (`chordWindow` on the step, editor field, default e.g. 2) | Each chord step sets its own W | chord (any W) | chord if W ≥ 1 | chord if W ≥ 3 | Most flexible; travels with the combo and share links. One more field per step in the editor, storage and share format. A per-combo setting is a middle ground (one field, but all chords in a combo share it). |

Sub-choice for any option: measure in grid frames (above) or in ms (e.g. "within W × 16.7 ms of the first press"). Ms avoids the frame-edge problem at W = 0 but doesn't match how games count.

## DECISION 2 — Which press's frame is graded

| Option | S2 (LP 10, MP 11) | S3 (LP 10, MP 13), if W ≥ 3 | Next step's reference frame | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2A. First press** | ON_TIME, 0f | ON_TIME, 0f | LP's frame (10) | Rewards starting the chord on time; the second press only has to be inside W. Result can't be shown until the chord completes or W runs out, though the timing is from the first press. |
| **2B. Last press (when the chord completes)** | ON_TIME, +1f | LATE, +3f | MP's frame (13) (S3) | Matches "the move happens when the chord is complete". A slow second press costs timing, which may feel harsh with a wide W. |
| **2C. Each press must be on time** | ON_TIME (both in 9–11) | LATE (MP is +3) | needs a rule: first or last | Strictest and most informative, but a step now has two deltas. Stats (average / spread use one `deltaFrames` per step) and feedback need a rule for which delta to show (e.g. the worst). This makes Q3 and Q4 real questions; under 2A/2B they mostly answer themselves. |

## DECISION 3 — First press too early, second inside the window (S4: LP 7, MP 10)

Only reachable as a chord if W ≥ 3; with a smaller W, S4 is a broken chord (→ Q5).

| Option | S4 result | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| **3A. Any press outside the on-time window fails the step** | EARLY, −3f ("LP 3f early") | Clear and strict. Same as 2C. Under 2B it overrides the "last press" rule for this case. |
| **3B. No special rule: whatever Q2 grades decides** | 2A → EARLY −3f; 2B → ON_TIME 0f (early LP only visible as a timeline dot) | No new rules. Under 2B a sloppy early first press is invisible in the grade, which may hide a habit you want to fix. |
| **3C. New outcome "split"** (chord straddles the window edge) | SPLIT: "LP 3f early, MP on time" | Most precise feedback. Adds a new `Grade` value: new color, sound choice, stats bucket, feedback text, and an engine API change. |

## DECISION 4 — First press early AND second press late (S5: LP 8 → −2f, MP 13 → +3f)

Only reachable if W is wider than the on-time window: one early and one late press are at least `tolEarly + tolLate + 2` frames apart (here 4). With W smaller than that, S5 is always a broken chord (→ Q5).

| Option | S5 result | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| **4A. First problem wins** | EARLY, −2f | Simple, follows time order. Hides that the second press was also off. |
| **4B. Bigger miss wins** (tie → earlier press) | LATE, +3f | Points at the worse habit. A rule you have to remember; ties need the tie-break. |
| **4C. "Split" outcome** (same new grade as 3C) | SPLIT: "LP 2f early, MP 3f late" | Most informative; same costs as 3C. Only worth it if 3C is chosen too. |

Note: whichever you pick, you could also make this case impossible by capping W at `tolEarly + tolLate + 1`. That cap would force W ≤ 1 on 0/0-tolerance steps, though.

## DECISION 5 — Wrong, partial or extra buttons

Common to all options: a partial chord (S6) is only known once W runs out, so its result appears W frames after the first press. The engine's `tick()` handles that, so it works even if no other press arrives.

| Option | S6 (LP only) | S7 (LP+HP) | S8 (LP+MP+HP) | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5A. Exact match; everything else is WRONG_BUTTON** | WRONG_BUTTON "LP only" | WRONG_BUTTON "LP+HP" | WRONG_BUTTON "LP+MP+HP" | No new grade; strict/non-strict work as today (non-strict: the presses are logged as ignored, the chord resets, and the step keeps waiting). Stats can't tell "dropped a button" from "pressed the wrong one". |
| **5B. Exact match, with a new PARTIAL grade for a subset** | PARTIAL "LP only, MP missing" | WRONG_BUTTON | WRONG_BUTTON | Stats and colors separate "half a chord" from "wrong button", which is the common chord mistake. Costs a new `Grade` value (format, colors, stats, sounds, timeline, tests). |
| **5C. Lenient: extra buttons are ignored, only missing ones fail** | WRONG_BUTTON (or PARTIAL) when W runs out | HP ignored and logged; still waits for MP within W | ON_TIME (HP logged as ignored) | Forgiving. Wrong for games where three buttons give a different move than two, and it hides an extra press. |

Also for any option: pressing the same chord button twice (LP, LP, MP) — treat the second LP as an extra press (same rule as S8).

## RESEARCH 6 — What has to change

### Step type ([src/engine/types.ts](../../src/engine/types.ts))
Recommended shape (open question below, since it's an engine API change):
```ts
interface Step {
  buttons: ButtonId[];      // 1 = a normal press, 2+ = a chord (order doesn't matter)
  targetFrame: number; tolEarly: number; tolLate: number; label?: string;
  chordWindow?: number;     // only if Decision 1 = per step
}
```
- Replaces `button` (one code path; a single button is a chord of one). Alternative: keep `button` and add an optional `chord` list. That keeps old data valid without migration, but every consumer then has to handle two fields.
- Saved combos: `parseStep` in [src/storage/combos.ts:202](../../src/storage/combos.ts#L202) reads an old `button` field as `buttons: [button]`, so no storage version bump.
- `StepResult`: `expected` becomes `ButtonId[]`; add `presses?: PressEvent[]` (every press in the chord, in order); keep `press` as the graded one. Per-press frames only if 2C/3C/4C are picked. `Grade` gains `PARTIAL` / `SPLIT` only if 3C/4C/5B are picked.
- `validateCombo` in [src/engine/attempt.ts:40](../../src/engine/attempt.ts#L40): `buttons` non-empty, no duplicates, no directions in a chord with 2+ buttons (directions are out of scope), `chordWindow` integer ≥ 0 if present.

### Grader / engine ([src/engine/attempt.ts](../../src/engine/attempt.ts))
- `AttemptState` gains a pending chord: the presses seen so far for the awaited step and the grid frame of the first one.
- `pressWhileRunning`: a chord button adds to the pending chord; complete → grade (Q2–Q4); a non-chord button or extra → Q5.
- `pressWhileArmed` (first-input start): a chord as step 1 must decide which press is frame 0 (open question).
- `tick()`: also closes a pending chord once W has run out (→ Q5 result).
- `lastInputFrame` (next step's reference) follows Q2.
- [src/engine/timing.ts](../../src/engine/timing.ts) `gradeFrames` is unchanged: it already grades a known frame count. Chord assembly is new logic, probably a small pure helper (e.g. `engine/chord.ts`).

### Editor ([src/ui/editor.ts:143](../../src/ui/editor.ts#L143), [src/combo/edit.ts](../../src/combo/edit.ts))
- The single button `<select>` becomes a button list: the first select plus "+ button" to add another and ✕ to remove one. A chord-window number field too, if Decision 1 = per step.
- On-time hint adds the chord rule, e.g. "LP+MP within 2 frames, frames 9 to 11…".
- `nextStep` copies `buttons`; `StepPatch`, `combosEqual` (line 79) and `updateStep` handle the array.
- [src/combo/share.ts](../../src/combo/share.ts): share code v2 (button field becomes a list), still decoding v1 links.
- [src/combo/examples.ts](../../src/combo/examples.ts): optionally one chord example.

### Timeline ([src/practice/timeline.ts](../../src/practice/timeline.ts), [src/ui/practice.ts](../../src/ui/practice.ts))
- `TimelineStep.input` (one dot) becomes one dot per chord press. `expected` shows as "LP+MP"; `pressed` becomes a list for wrong chords.
- Optionally shade the chord window after the first press.
- The ignored-press lane is unchanged.

### Other touch points
- [src/practice/format.ts](../../src/practice/format.ts): "Wrong button: LP+HP", "LP only", and split/partial text if chosen.
- [src/practice/stats.ts](../../src/practice/stats.ts): unchanged if a step keeps one `deltaFrames` (2A/2B). Needs a rule under 2C. New buckets if new grades are added.
- [src/ui/practice.ts](../../src/ui/practice.ts): `stepName(step.button…)`, step cards, stats table, status line ("Press LP+MP to start").
- [src/practice/cues.ts](../../src/practice/cues.ts): metronome and Hear timing are per step, so no change. Step sounds fire when the step is graded, which for a chord is when it completes (or W runs out).
- Input layer: **no change.** Chord presses are ordinary separate press events. Known limits to document: some keyboards can't register certain 3+ key combinations (rollover/ghosting), and pad buttons seen in the same poll share a timestamp.

## Proposed SPEC changes (applied once you've decided; `[Qn]` = filled from your answer)

**§3 Combo definition**, replace the `button` bullet:
> - `buttons`: the input(s) to press. One button is a normal step. Two or more is a **chord**: all of them must be pressed within the chord window, in any order. Directions can't be part of a chord (yet).
> - `chordWindow` [Q1, only if per step]: how many grid frames apart a chord's presses may be.

**§3 Grading**, add after rule 4:
> **Chords.** A chord's presses are collected from the first chord button pressed. The chord is complete when every button has been pressed within [Q1] frames of the first. The graded frame is [Q2]. If presses fall on both sides of the on-time window: [Q3, Q4]. Wrong, missing or extra buttons: [Q5]. A chord's result is known when it completes or when its window runs out. The next relative step counts from [Q2 reference].

**§3 Input rules**, add:
> - A chord's presses are ordinary press edges; no input-layer change. Strict mode applies to chord mistakes as described above.

**§4 Architecture:** add `chord.ts` under `engine/` if it ends up separate.

**§9 Out of scope:** change "simultaneous multi-button presses" to "directions in chords, negative edge, motion inputs".

**Backlog:** mark the chord part of "Grouped inputs" as in progress; add the later items above.

**Decisions log**, new entry "Chords (2026-10-31)": your answers to Q1–Q5, the `buttons` type change and old-data migration, share code v2, the first-input chord rule, and these simplest-option defaults (not on your DECISION list, so per CLAUDE.md I'd pick the simplest and log them):
- The MISSED window is counted from the chord's first press.
- A non-strict mistake resets the pending chord.
- The same chord button pressed twice counts as an extra press.
